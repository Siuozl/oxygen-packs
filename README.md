# Oxygen knowledge packs

Pack files for [Oxygen](https://oxygen-psm.web.app). The app downloads the packs a person chooses and keeps them on their device.

The encyclopedia text comes from [Simple English Wikipedia](https://simple.wikipedia.org) and [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org) (shortened articles) and is available under the [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) licence. Each entry keeps the title of its article.
